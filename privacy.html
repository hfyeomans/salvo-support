Privacy Policy

Salvo: Naval Duel for iPhone · Effective 8 November 2026

Salvo does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your settings (sound, voice, haptics, difficulty, rules) and your career record (games played, wins, best accuracy) are saved only on your device using the standard iOS preferences store. They never leave your phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or server. The app works entirely offline.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No third-party code receives any data.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or the network.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no data, no information is collected from children or anyone else.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that needs data (for example online play), this policy will be updated here before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
