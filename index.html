Salvo: Naval Duel

Support for the iPhone app

Getting started

Salvo is a guess-and-sink naval duel for iPhone, designed around iPhone Duo's folding display: enemy waters on the top screen, your fleet on the bottom.

  • Quick battle: Play › Randomize › Start Battle.
  • Placing ships: drag a ship to move it, tap it to rotate.
  • Classic vs Salvo rules: Classic is one shot per turn. In Salvo you fire one shot for every ship you still have afloat.
  • Pass and play: choose the two-player opponent. Fold the phone between turns and the boards cover themselves.
  • Sound, voice and haptics can each be switched off from the main menu.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound: check the ring/silent switch and that Sound effects is on in the menu.
  • No haptics: check Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics, and that Haptics is on in the menu.
  • Boards not side by side: the split layout needs iPhone Duo's inner display. On other iPhones, use the Attack/Fleet switch above the board.
  • Reset career stats: delete and reinstall the app. Stats are stored only on your phone.
